About HappyInvite

HappyInvite is your all-in-one digital platform to design, customize, and send stunning greetings and invitation cards for every special occasion in life. Whether it is birthdays, weddings, new year celebrations, or milestone achievements, HappyInvite makes spreading joy simple and memorable.

Our Mission

We believe every celebration deserves a personal touch. Our mission is to provide intuitive, customizable design tools combined with effortless sharing options so everyone can express their warmest wishes with ease.

Features & Capabilities

  • 🎨 Highly customizable greeting and invitation card templates
  • 📱 Mobile-friendly drag-and-drop design editors
  • 💌 Full-stack sync across your saved templates and designs
  • 🚀 Instant digital download and print options
